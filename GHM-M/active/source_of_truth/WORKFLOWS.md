## WF-001: MRD Version Lifecycle

**Workflow Name:** MRD Version Lifecycle
**Category:** Methodology Development
**Status:** Active
**Created:** 2025-12-26

### Overview
Progressive lifecycle for methodology development from Spark (v0.1) to Evolution (v1.0).

### Stages
1. **v0.1 Spark:** Problem, vision, scope
2. **v0.2 Practitioner Definition:** Target audiences, pain points
3. **v0.3 Adoption Model:** How methodology spreads
4. **v0.4 Practitioner Journeys:** Adoption paths
5. **v0.5 Validation:** Risk assessment, validation planning
6. **v0.6 Methodology Structure:** Components, patterns, templates
7. **v0.7 Documentation Creation:** Build artifacts
8. **v0.8 Publication:** Distribution strategy
9. **v0.9 Community Building:** Adoption & engagement
10. **v1.0 Evolution:** Real-world refinement

### Gates
Each version has specific deliverables and validation criteria.
