### Example (Filled)

```markdown
## Strategic Context

**Last Updated:** 2024-01-26
**Owner:** Jessica Chen (VP Product)
**Review Frequency:** Monthly

### Vision

**Product Vision:**
Enable small businesses to compete with enterprises through AI-powered automation

**Mission:**
Democratize powerful automation tools that were previously only accessible to large companies with dedicated IT teams

**Long-Term Goal (18-24 months):**
Become the #1 automation platform for SMBs (10-100 employees), with 10,000 paying customers and category leadership

### Market & Opportunity

**Market Opportunity:**
- TAM: $10B (all SMB automation software)
- SAM: $2B (no-code automation for 10-100 person companies)
- SOM: $100M (realistic 3-year capture in US market)
- Market growth rate: 25% YoY

**Key Market Trends:**
1. AI adoption accelerating in SMB segment (45% planning AI investments)
2. No-code tools reducing technical barriers (market growing 40% YoY)
3. Economic pressure driving automation urgency (labor costs up 15%)

### Target Customers

**Primary Customer Segment:**
- Profile: Operations managers at 10-100 person professional services firms
- Size: ~5M potential customers in US
- Willingness to pay: $75-150 per user per month
- Current alternative: Manual processes, basic Zapier, disconnected point solutions

**Customer Pain Points:**
1. **Manual data entry**: 10+ hours/week copying data between systems
2. **Can't afford enterprise tools**: Salesforce automation costs $300+/user/month
3. **No technical staff**: Can't hire developers to build custom automation

**Customer Persona (Primary):**
- Name: "Busy Beth"
- Role: Operations Manager at 50-person accounting firm
- Day-to-day: Managing client onboarding, data entry, reporting
- Pain: Drowning in repetitive tasks, working nights and weekends
- Goal: Automate 80% of manual work without learning to code
- Quote: "I need automation that just works - I don't have time to become a programmer"

### Positioning & Differentiation

**Value Proposition:**
For operations managers at SMBs who waste hours on manual data tasks, we're an AI-powered automation platform that eliminates repetitive work with no coding required. Unlike Zapier which is broad but shallow, we provide enterprise-grade reliability specifically for SMB workflows.

**Competitive Landscape:**
| Competitor | Strength | Weakness | How We Win |
|------------|----------|----------|------------|
| Zapier | Broad integrations (5000+) | Reliability issues, shallow features | Enterprise-grade reliability, SMB-focused |
| Make.com | Power user features | Too complex for SMBs | No-code simplicity with power when needed |

**Competitive Moat:**
- Purpose-built CRM integration depth (vs generic connectors)
- SMB-specific templates and workflows (vs generic)
- Price point optimized for SMB budgets

### Business Model

**Monetization:**
- Pricing model: Per-user subscription
- Price point: $99 per user per month
- Average contract value (ACV): $5,940 (5 users, annual)
- Lifetime value (LTV): $17,820 (3-year avg retention)

**Unit Economics:**
- CAC: $1,200
- LTV:CAC ratio: 14.9:1
- Gross margin: 85%
- Payback period: 2.4 months

### Strategic Priorities

**Current Quarter (Q1 2024):**
1. **Launch MVP**: Workflow builder + 2 CRM integrations
2. **Validate pricing**: 100 paying customers at $99/user
3. **Prove reliability**: 99.9% uptime, <2s execution

**Not Doing:**
- Not building mobile apps (desktop-first, mobile v2)
- Not adding 20 integrations (focus on depth with 2 CRMs first)
- Not enterprise sales (staying SMB-focused)

### Success Metrics

**North Star Metric:**
Active workflows running per customer (indicates value delivery)

**Key Metrics:**
| Metric | Current | Target (Q1) | Target (Year) |
|--------|---------|-------------|---------------|
| Paying customers | 12 | 100 | 1,000 |
| MRR | $7K | $60K | $600K |
| Active workflows | 45 | 500 | 10,000 |

---
```

**Naming:**

- Hooks: camelCase with 'use' prefix (useAuth)

- Strict mode: enabled
- Prefer `interface` over `type` for objects
- No `any` - use `unknown` when uncertain
- Export types with implementations

**React:**
- Functional components only
- Hooks for state and effects
- Props destructured in signature
- Event handlers: `handle*` prefix
