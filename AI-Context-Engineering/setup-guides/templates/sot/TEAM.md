## Cross-Functional Ownership (RACI)

### RACI Matrix for Key Decisions

**R = Responsible** (does the work)
**A = Accountable** (ultimate decision maker)
**C = Consulted** (provides input)
**I = Informed** (kept in loop)

| Decision/Activity | Product | Engineering | Design | Marketing | Sales/CS |
|-------------------|---------|-------------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Product Strategy** | A, R | C | C | C | C |
| **Feature Prioritization** | A, R | C | C | I | C |
| **Technical Architecture** | C | A, R | I | I | I |
| **UX/UI Design** | C | C | A, R | I | I |
| **Launch Planning** | A, R | C | C | R | C |
| **Pricing Decisions** | A | I | I | C | C |
| **GTM Strategy** | C | I | I | A, R | C |
| **Customer Feedback** | C | C | C | I | A, R |
| **Support Policies** | C | C | I | I | A, R |

## Team Norms & Processes

### Communication

**Primary Tools:**
- Slack: Daily communication
- Email: External communication
- [Project Tool]: Task tracking
- [Meeting Tool]: Video calls

**Slack Channels:**
- #general: Company-wide announcements
- #product: Product discussions
- #engineering: Technical discussions
- #design: Design reviews
- #launch: Launch coordination
- #customer-feedback: Customer insights
- #metrics: Data and analytics

**Response Expectations:**
- Urgent (Slack @mention): < 1 hour
- Normal (Slack message): < 4 hours
- Email: < 24 hours

### Meetings

**Standing Meetings:**

**Daily Standup (15 min)** — During launch week
- When: 9:30 AM daily
- Who: Full team
- Format: Blockers, priorities, quick updates

**Weekly Product Sync (60 min)**
- When: [Day/Time]
- Who: Product, Eng Leads, Design Lead
- Agenda: Roadmap, priorities, dependencies

**Weekly Team All-Hands (30 min)**
- When: [Day/Time]
- Who: Full team
- Agenda: Wins, metrics, priorities, announcements

**Sprint Planning (2 hours)** — Bi-weekly
- When: [Day/Time]
- Who: Product, Engineering, Design
- Agenda: Next sprint scope and estimates

**Sprint Retro (1 hour)** — Bi-weekly
- When: [Day/Time]
- Who: Full team
- Agenda: What went well, what didn't, action items

### Decision-Making Process

**Small Decisions** (Day-to-day execution)
- Decided by: Responsible party (per RACI)
- Document: In issue/PR/Slack thread
- Notify: Relevant stakeholders

**Medium Decisions** (Feature scope, technical choices)
- Decided by: Accountable party (per RACI)
- Process: Consult relevant parties → Decide → Document
- Document: DECISIONS.md (DEC-XXX)
- Notify: #product or #engineering channel

**Large Decisions** (Strategic, significant impact)
- Decided by: Leadership team
- Process: Gather input → Written proposal → Discussion → Decide
- Document: PRD.md or DECISIONS.md
- Notify: #general (all-hands)

### Launch Protocol

**During Launch Week:**
- Daily standup at 9:30 AM (all team)
- Dedicated #launch Slack channel
- 24/7 on-call coverage (rotating)
- Immediate escalation for P0 issues
- Daily metrics review at 5 PM
